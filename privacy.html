Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

EMCO Drum Shells (“EMCO,” “we”) builds drum shells in Kansas City, USA. This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit this website or contact us.

What this website collects

This website is informational. It has no forms, accounts, or shopping cart, so there’s nowhere on it to enter personal information. It doesn’t use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

When you call or email us

If you contact us by phone or email, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, and what you’d like to order. We use them to answer you, handle your order, and look after you as a customer. We don’t sell your personal information.

Fonts from Google

The typefaces on this site are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the fonts from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and basic browser information as part of that request. Google’s use of that information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Website hosting

Like most websites, this site is delivered by a hosting provider whose servers may automatically record standard request details, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. These records are used to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Links to other websites

This site links to social media and other websites we don’t control. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email info@emcodrumshells.com or call 816-300-1550.
